# Slippy Business Model

Version: 1.0

---

# Executive Summary

Slippy is not a Receipt OCR application.

Slippy is an AI Life Assistant Platform.

The business model should not rely on OCR usage fees.

The long-term goal is to build a recurring revenue platform powered by:

* Subscription
* Business SaaS
* Financial Marketplace
* Creator Economy

Creator Economy is designed primarily as a Growth Engine, not the main Revenue Engine.

# Unit Economics Principles

Every new feature must satisfy:

1. Increase Revenue
2. Increase Retention
3. Increase WALU
4. Enrich Life Graph

If a feature satisfies none of the above, reconsider implementation.
